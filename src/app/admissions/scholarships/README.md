Precedence: the amended schedule overrides the older category text where they conflict (notably JEE Main 2,000 vs 20,000 and Perambalur renewal-only). Keep ambiguous published boundaries explicit: employee service anniversary gaps, SAT “1501 above”, and UG “Pharm Programme” wording. General exclusions include NEET programmes, Pharm.D and Pharm.D (PB); external sponsored schemes follow their own rules. Sports exam-fee coverage is not asserted because its schedule cell is blank. Exact threshold comparison signs matter (general renewals use >; faculty policy lists CGPA 8 or 7).
